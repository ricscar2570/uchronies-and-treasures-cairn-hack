# Setting

Las Vegas, 2080 is a functioning city built around a disaster that never stopped happening. Use the setting in two layers:

- **Las Vegas, 2080** gives every participant the public facts, districts, institutions, daily life, technology, and campaign tensions needed to understand the city.
- **Adventure Sites** gives the Warden situation-driven locations, nodes, clocks, factions, and consequences ready for missions.

The setting does not require a single canonical conspiracy. Project Oculus has established facts and open questions. Choose answers through evidence and play, then let those answers complicate the people and institutions the agents already know.
