# Design & Hacking Companion

This separate companion preserves material removed from the core game manual to improve its focus, pacing, and usability at the table.

It contains the extended Frequently Asked Questions, design commentary, Monte Carlo methodology, and guidance for creating derivative hacks. None of these chapters is required to learn or run *Uchronies & Treasures*.

The rules text and table procedures remain authoritative in the Core Edition. Where a companion explanation appears to conflict with an operative rule, follow the operative rule.
