# Omnibus Edition

This profile gathers every unique reader-facing component of the Step 22 line into one A5 two-column book. It deliberately does **not** append the Operational profile as a second copy of the rules: Operational is an alternate publication profile whose content is already represented by the core chapters, adventures, and table tools included here.

Included: full core rules, Las Vegas and adventure-site material, Warden procedures, both adventures, the full Example of Play, all table/reference tools, Design Notes, FAQ, Monte Carlo transparency, hacking guide, glossary, rule index, and credits.

Before the omnibus build, the four P1 cross-volume discrepancies identified in the Step 22 audit were corrected: information-layer terminology, the six-step Adjudication Ladder, starting-package slot wording, and Chicago Loop local-event route-change ambiguity. Two P2 clarifications were also incorporated: the harsher-zone contamination timing at Deterioration 3 and an occupied-now field on the Meridian operations sheet.
