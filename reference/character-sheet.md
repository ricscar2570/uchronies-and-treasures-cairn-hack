# Character and Table Sheets

The R2.2.1 packet includes three separate A4 printable, fillable sheets. They are play aids, not extra rule systems. Download the complete packet from the development checkpoint; the sheet filenames are stable within it.

**Character sheet:** identity, current/max STR, DEX, WIL and HP, Armor and temporal protection, Loyalty and Corruption, five playable Quirk entries, ten inventory slots, weapon/ammunition state, exposure fraction and personal ties. The sixth Quirk means an echo, not another playable slot. Cash, Debt and Certificates live on the accounting sheet.

**Accounting sheet:** start-of-week rank, Loyalty and Instability; income, paid costs, unpaid costs, voluntary repayment, clemency and closing Debt; Cash and Certificates remain separate. There is a transaction/obligations area for pay conditions, independent sales, Raines jobs and disputed claims. No inferred debt is added without an entry.

**Warden mission sheet:** objectives and band criteria, costs and payer, incompatible interests, NPC knowledge and motives, retreats, zone/clock transitions, extra hazards, faction consequences and debrief. Its time log separates fictional hours from real session time.

Files: `CHRONOCAIRN_R2_2_1_Character_Sheet.pdf`, `CHRONOCAIRN_R2_2_1_Accounting_Sheet.pdf`, `CHRONOCAIRN_R2_2_1_Warden_Mission_Sheet.pdf`. Source generator: `scripts/build_sheets_r22.py`.

The Accounting Sheet is manually filled, not an automatic calculator: reject repayments above available Cash or principal. All three sheets are also appended, at original A4 size, to the digital Omnibus. After filling, save a copy and reopen it before printing.
