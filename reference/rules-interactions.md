# Rules Interactions and Edge Cases

**Damage and Scars.** Subtract Armor first, minimum 0. A single attack reducing positive HP to exactly 0 indexes Scars by damage taken, capped at 12. Do not roll d12. Overflow damages STR and tests reduced current STR instead. Inventory exhaustion is not an attack and grants no Scar.

**Initiative.** A successful opening DEX save grants a turn before the first enemy action. Then enemies and all PCs alternate. Failure delays the opening action; it does not remove the turn following the first enemy action. If Quirks impose both automatic opening success and automatic failure, they cancel for that opening: roll the normal DEX save.

**Two protections.** Armor protects HP against ordinary attacks; temporal suits reduce contamination damage to WIL. Neither substitutes for the other. A heavy suit prevents damage-based Quirks from d4 exposure, not WIL erosion. Named physical-shock hazards follow their own stated procedure.

**Clock boundaries.** Carry fractions across zones. If a periodic check falls exactly at exit, resolve it first. Ending a scene, extracting or selling an item creates no check unless a named handling hazard says so. Extra checks do not reset the periodic clock. Eight uninterrupted Green-rest hours clear residual exposure, not WIL loss.

**Recovery.** Ordinary HP rest, attribute care, medicine supply and clearing the Exposure Clock are separate. Contamination WIL restores only on completion of its stated treatment, up to maximum WIL, never while Deprived. No recovery happens just because the session ended. Routine medical kits restore STR, not WIL. An effect that explicitly restores contamination damage does not implicitly remove permanent Quirks or reverse an echo.

**Six trackers, separate rank.** Loyalty, Corruption, Quirks, Cash, Debt and Certificates are the six trackers. Rank, current/max attributes, inventory, exposure fractions and dated obligations are recorded separately. The number of completed information sales is read from the transaction log; it is not temporal Exposure.

**Pay.** One field allowance and one band per completed field assignment. At Loyalty 7+, one successful band per week improves by one step, never above Exceptional. At Loyalty 9+, only base pay rises by 50%. No uplift to failure or a replacement bounty. Use pre-reward Loyalty for mission benefits and start-of-week status for payroll. Instability suspends special benefits, not ordinary wages.

**Allegiances.** Raines is not Zhou. Every second Raines job costs -1 Loyalty; no automatic Corruption. Instability needs both trackers at least 3 and a difference no greater than 2. Its social disadvantage concerns risky saves with informed, involved factions. A third unstable close creates a crisis, not compulsory allegiance.

**Reloads and replacement.** A loaded gun still fires with no spares. One spare costs $50; two spares use one slot, rounded up. A replacement PC starts with their own $500 Debt and normal starting Cash, not an inherited personal balance. Explicit team obligations remain recorded without silently assigning a new debtor.
