## Payout and Pressure Reminders

Ordinary mission totals per agent: failure $50; standard $190; strong $260; exceptional $400. These already include the $50 allowance and the 30% performance deduction. A bounty replaces the performance band. A specifically briefed Trusted assignment may have the $200 gross adjustment described in Economy. Hero Loyalty 9-10 changes base salary only, subject to the benefit lock.

Zhou's Debt thresholds are $700, $1,300, $2,200 and $4,000. Record an offer or scene, not automatic acceptance or automatic Corruption. A simulation reserve of $200 is not a rule requiring the player to repay Debt.

A certificate cash-out gives $800 per certificate and +1 Corruption for the transaction, not per certificate. Record the actual number surrendered and the single transaction.
