## Release Rule

Before a commercial or public production release, every row must be changed to one of:

- **Original and documented:** creator, date, source file, and ownership recorded;
- **Licensed:** licensor, exact license or signed agreement, permitted media and modifications, attribution recorded;
- **Public domain:** reliable source and public-domain basis recorded;
- **Removed or replaced:** uncleared file deleted from repository and generated outputs.

Do not infer permission from possession of a file, prior inclusion in a PDF, lack of metadata, or the fact that the surrounding manuscript is openly licensed.

## Recommended Record Fields

For every final asset, record:

- repository path and final publication filename;
- title or description;
- creator and rights holder;
- source URL, invoice, contract, or original-file reference;
- license or grant date;
- print, PDF, web, advertising, crowdfunding, social-media, translation, modification, and derivative rights;
- exclusivity and territory, if any;
- required credit wording;
- restrictions or expiration;
- confirmation that model, property, trademark, or privacy releases are unnecessary or obtained.

This ledger records uncertainty; it does not assert ownership or grant reuse rights.
